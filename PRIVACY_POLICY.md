# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 26, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Al-Minshawi Telawat** application ("the App") handles user information. The App is developed by **Islam Alorabi** and is available on the Google Play Store.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to operate with minimal data usage and does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.** The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of user registration.

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party:

- **App Preferences:** Language selection, theme mode (light/dark/system), and playback settings such as repeat mode, auto-play, and sleep timer configuration.
- **Playback State:** The last played surah and playback position, so you can resume where you left off.
- **Favorites:** Your list of favorite surahs.
- **Cached Audio Files:** Downloaded Quran recitation audio files stored in the app's internal cache for offline listening.
- **Scroll Position:** Your current scroll position in the surah list.

## Internet Usage

The App requires an internet connection solely for:

- **Streaming** Quran recitation audio from the hosting server.
- **Downloading** Quran recitation audio files for offline playback.

No personal data is transmitted over the internet. The App does not make any other network requests.

## Third-Party Services

The App does **not** use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

- Analytics or tracking tools
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting services
- Social media integrations
- Cloud storage or databases

## Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Internet** | Required to stream and download Quran audio recitations. |
| **Wake Lock** | Keeps the device awake during audio playback to prevent interruptions. |
| **Foreground Service (Media Playback)** | Allows audio playback to continue in the background via a media notification. |

## Data Sharing

We do **not** share, sell, trade, or transfer any user data to any third parties. Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to share.

## Data Security

All app data is stored locally on your device using Android's standard storage mechanisms. Cached audio files are stored in the app's private internal storage directory, accessible only to the App itself.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App contains Quran recitations only and is suitable for all ages.

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

- Clearing the app's cache and data from your device's Settings.
- Uninstalling the App, which removes all associated data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
